autoplotly v0.1.5
---------------------

* Restored composition with `ggplot2` elements under `ggplot2` 4.0 on R 4.3
  and later.
* Documented the list-based composition workaround for `ggplot2` 4.0 on
  R 4.1 and R 4.2. Compatibility with `ggplot2` 3.x is unchanged.
